3D Enclosure

This page collects community 3D enclosure and device design projects based on the Wio Tracker L1 Series.

L1​

Magnetic Wireless Charger​

Source: PocketMesh Bank - an everyday power bank + Meshtastic node

It integrates Wio Tracker L1 into a compact magnetic wireless power bank, enabling phone charging, node power supply, and a more portable antenna-integrated design.

Who made it: Saheen Palayi

Video: PocketMesh Bank

Where to download: a360

WioFold Communicator​

Source: The Wio Tracker L1 Meshtastic Communicator

A foldable Wio Tracker L1-based Meshtastic communicator with a physical keyboard and replaceable 18650 battery.

Who made it: Dávid Czipó

Ride Trace​

Source: LoRider- the Meshatastic

A compact bike-mounted Meshtastic tracker built on Wio Tracker L1, designed for GPS tracking, anti-theft alerts, front light functionality, and discreet everyday riding use.

Who made it: Miriam Choi

Wio Tracker L1 Gateway​

Source: Wio Tracker L1 Gateway

A hybrid Meshtastic gateway based on Wio Tracker L1, designed to collect environmental telemetry, relay it across the mesh, and bridge it to the cloud via MQTT for real-time remote monitoring.

Who made it: Hendra Kusumah

A Self-Sustaining Meshtastic Node Family​

Source: SEEEDpod Ultimate

A modular Meshtastic node ecosystem built around Wio Tracker L1, designed for off-grid deployment, urban portability, and everyday backup use with self-sustaining power options.

Who made it: Jack

Dual Wio Tracker L1 Portable Solar Station​

Source: Dual Solar Charging Station for Wio Tracker L1

A rugged portable solar station designed to charge two Wio Tracker L1 nodes anywhere, while also enabling Grove sensor integration for off-grid monitoring and emergency communication.

Who made it: Fernando Doutel

E-ink​

Meshy: A Rugged 3D-Printed Case for Wio Tracker L1 E-ink​

Source: meshy-wio-tracker-l1-e-ink-3d-printed-case-affad1

This is a 3D-printed protective case for the Wio Tracker L1 E-ink, designed to improve durability, portability, and mounting convenience for outdoor and everyday carry use. Its parametric design supports different battery sizes and mounting structures, and includes a clip-on mount for belts, backpacks, or MOLLE gear.

Who made it: Akash

Where to download: a360

MeshPack Pro L1​

Source: [ACOS] MeshPack Pro L1 for Meshtastic

A rugged, modular 3D-printed enclosure for Wio Tracker L1, designed to turn the board into a more durable and customizable handheld Meshtastic device.

Who made it: Alley Cat

Solar Off-Grid Mesh Communicator​

Source: Meshtastic 20.25

A self-contained off-grid Meshtastic communicator built around Wio Tracker L1, combining a mini keyboard, E-ink display, vibration alerts, and rear solar charging for portable everyday and outdoor use.

Who made it: Bastiaan Slee

Lite​

SkySense Flying Lab​

Source: SkySense - The Perfect Flying Laboratory

A balloon-borne flying laboratory built with Wio Tracker L1, combining environmental sensing, GPS tracking, image capture, and LoRa telemetry for low-cost aerial experiments.

Who made it: pasquale887
