# CodeCraft for Education

CodeCraft is committed to combining cutting-edge AI “Vibe Coding” with open-source hardware to provide schools, training institutions, and teachers with a truly low-barrier, zero-code maker education and AI programming solution. It integrates software, hardware, and curriculum into a unified platform, significantly reducing both lesson preparation effort for teachers and the learning barrier for students.  

For classroom use, it is recommended to use **Classroom Code Login**, enabling students to enter the creative environment quickly and seamlessly.

![Educational Scenario](https://files.seeedstudio.com/SenseCraft/sensecraft-wiki/codecraft/4-codecraft-for-education/Educational-Scenario-EN.png)

---

## 4.1 What We Provide

We offer a complete, one-stop AI maker education solution for schools, eliminating all unnecessary setup work:

* **Zero-configuration AI programming platform**: Web-based and ready to use instantly. Supports mainstream browsers such as Chrome and Edge. No software installation, environment setup, or driver installation required for school computer labs.
* **Plug-and-play hardware kits**: Fully compatible with Seeed Studio flagship hardware (Grove Beginner Kit, Wio Terminal, XIAO ESP32-S3, etc.). Plug-in connections eliminate complex wiring.
* **Ready-to-use maker curriculum resources**: STEM project-based learning content from beginner to advanced levels, including rich teaching cases and workshop guides, allowing teachers to start teaching immediately.
* **Dedicated school management console**: Teachers have access to an independent admin panel to generate classroom-specific “course codes” with one click, making it easy to manage class size and compute resources.

---

## 4.2 How to Collaborate With Us

We offer flexible cooperation and procurement models. Whether for a single classroom or a regional deployment, integration is simple:

* **Model A: Direct school/institution procurement**  
  Schools or maker spaces purchase CodeCraft education seat packages (including AI compute quotas) and optionally acquire Seeed Studio hardware kits. Teachers can directly use the platform for daily teaching.

* **Model B: Channel partner bulk cooperation**  
  Education distributors can purchase seats and compute packages in bulk, redistribute or resell them to partner schools and training institutions. The system provides independent management links to ensure data isolation between institutions.

* **After-class extension (student self-upgrade)**  
  Students participate in classroom sessions for free using teacher-issued course codes. After class, students who wish to continue exploring or build personal maker projects can register and upgrade to individual plans (Starter / Plus).

---

## 4.3 Benefits for Teachers

Say goodbye to the traditional pain of hardware programming classes where half the lesson is spent installing drivers and configuring environments:

* **Zero preparation burden**: No need to master complex C++ or Python syntax. Teaching shifts from memorizing code to cultivating creativity and problem-solving skills.
* **Simplified classroom management**: Before class, teachers generate a **course code** in the admin panel and share it with students. No need to create accounts or manage passwords for dozens of students.
* **Secure and controllable resources**: Course codes can be disabled after class, fully locking AI compute resources and preventing misuse or unauthorized consumption.
* **Full-service support**: We provide continuous offline workshops, monitor teaching data in real time, and respond quickly to educational needs.

---

## 4.4 Benefits for Students

Let every student turn ideas in their mind into reality instantly:

> 💡 **Core Experience Flow:**
> 1. **Teacher generates code**: The teacher configures a class in the backend (e.g., “2-hour session, up to 50 students”) and generates a course code.
> 2. **Students join class**: Students enter CodeCraft using the course code and immediately begin AI creation and project generation.
> 3. **One-click flashing experience**: Connect hardware via USB, click “One-click Flash” in the browser, and deploy the program directly to the device to see it run instantly.
> 4. **Session release**: After class ends, students are automatically logged out and seats are released for the next session.
