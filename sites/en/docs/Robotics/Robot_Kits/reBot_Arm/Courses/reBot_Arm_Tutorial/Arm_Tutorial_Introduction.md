# Seeed Embodied Intelligence Beginner's Course: 8 Stages, 40 Chapters

This course was written and shared for free by the engineers of the Seeed robotics team. Our goal is to share knowledge and provide a systematic learning path for those who feel lost. You are welcome to invite friends to study together. However, free sharing does not mean piracy, copy-and-paste for profit, or misappropriation of others' work. The copyright belongs to Seeed Studio (Shenzhen) Co., Ltd. We will protect our rights against any unauthorized copyright infringement.

**Cross-disciplinary robotics learners, new graduate students building technical reserves, job seekers expanding their knowledge, and makers wanting to accelerate their own projects — no need to scavenge materials everywhere!** This permanently free course, carefully crafted by the Seeed Studio AI Robotics team, pairs with the 100% open-source, commercially usable, and reproducible **reBot** robotic arm. Finish this course and you'll be the next expert!

This course is completely free. If you'd like to encourage our efforts, please star our open-source robotic arm on GitHub: [Seeed-Projects/reBot-DevArm](https://github.com/Seeed-Projects/reBot-DevArm) ⭐, and help more people discover this course!

If you are already an expert, you can directly reference our wiki to start running examples: [Robotics Page](https://wiki.seeedstudio.com/robotics_page/).

Hardware drawings and BOM downloads for the robotic arm can be found on our GitHub: [Seeed-Projects/reBot-DevArm](https://github.com/Seeed-Projects/reBot-DevArm).

This course does not delve deeply into mathematical functions or low-level principles. It uses popular science and examples to help you quickly understand low-level control, traditional algorithms, and VLA algorithms in robotic arm embodied intelligence. Through a combination of theory and practice, you will comprehensively learn cutting-edge knowledge of robotic arms. If you want to go deeper into each field, many excellent platforms and instructors offer more advanced course content. Feel free to pursue targeted advanced study.
