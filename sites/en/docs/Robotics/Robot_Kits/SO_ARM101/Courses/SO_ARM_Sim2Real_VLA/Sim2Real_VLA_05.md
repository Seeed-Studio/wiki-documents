In this module, you explored the complete embodied-AI workflow that will be
used throughout this course:

- **LeRobot** provides the software foundation — data collection, policy
  training, deployment, and evaluation in one framework, backed by the
  Hugging Face community;
- **Sim-to-Real** explains why simulation is the scaling tool of modern
  robot learning, and why the gap between simulation and reality is the
  central engineering problem;
- the **SO-ARM101** is the hardware platform for every experiment ahead: a
  leader arm for human input and a follower arm that executes — the same
  embodiment NVIDIA's SO-101 workshop and reference checkpoints target.

Now that you understand the overall workflow, it is time to prepare the
physical system.
