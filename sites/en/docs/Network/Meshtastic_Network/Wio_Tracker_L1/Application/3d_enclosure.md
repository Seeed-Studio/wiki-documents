# 3D Enclosure

This page collects community 3D enclosure and device design projects based on the Wio Tracker L1 Series.

## L1

### Magnetic Wireless Charger

<p style={{textAlign: 'center'}}><img src="https://files.seeedstudio.com/wiki/wiki%20images/3d%20Enclosure/6.23dimage1.png" alt="Magnetic Wireless Charger" width={800} height="auto" /></p>

<p style={{textAlign: 'center', fontSize: '0.85rem'}}><strong>Source:</strong> <a href="https://www.hackster.io/saheenpalayi/pocketmesh-bank-an-everyday-power-bank-meshtastic-node-20202b" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">PocketMesh Bank - an everyday power bank + Meshtastic node</a></p>

It integrates Wio Tracker L1 into a compact magnetic wireless power bank, enabling phone charging, node power supply, and a more portable antenna-integrated design.

**Who made it:** [Saheen Palayi](https://www.hackster.io/saheenpalayi)

**Video:** [PocketMesh Bank](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gsW4RvVaC_4)

**Where to download:** [a360](https://a360.co/4nJFKRY)

### WioFold Communicator

<p style={{textAlign: 'center'}}><img src="https://files.seeedstudio.com/wiki/wiki%20images/3d%20Enclosure/6.23dimage2.png" alt="WioFold Communicator" width={800} height="auto" /></p>

<p style={{textAlign: 'center', fontSize: '0.85rem'}}><strong>Source:</strong> <a href="https://www.hackster.io/czipod/the-wio-tracker-l1-meshtastic-communicator-9465eb" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">The Wio Tracker L1 Meshtastic Communicator</a></p>

A foldable Wio Tracker L1-based Meshtastic communicator with a physical keyboard and replaceable 18650 battery.

**Who made it:** [Dávid Czipó](https://www.hackster.io/czipod)

### Ride Trace

<p style={{textAlign: 'center'}}><img src="https://files.seeedstudio.com/wiki/wiki%20images/3d%20Enclosure/6.23dimage3.png" alt="Ride Trace" width={800} height="auto" /></p>

<p style={{textAlign: 'center', fontSize: '0.85rem'}}><strong>Source:</strong> <a href="https://www.hackster.io/Milliam/lorider-the-meshatastic-0cffeb" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">LoRider- the Meshatastic</a></p>

A compact bike-mounted Meshtastic tracker built on Wio Tracker L1, designed for GPS tracking, anti-theft alerts, front light functionality, and discreet everyday riding use.

**Who made it:** [Miriam Choi](https://www.hackster.io/Milliam)

### Wio Tracker L1 Gateway

<p style={{textAlign: 'center'}}><img src="https://files.seeedstudio.com/wiki/wiki%20images/3d%20Enclosure/6.23dimage4.png" alt="Wio Tracker L1 Gateway" width={800} height="auto" /></p>

<p style={{textAlign: 'center', fontSize: '0.85rem'}}><strong>Source:</strong> <a href="https://www.hackster.io/hendra/wio-tracker-l1-gateway-9421c2" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Wio Tracker L1 Gateway</a></p>

A hybrid Meshtastic gateway based on Wio Tracker L1, designed to collect environmental telemetry, relay it across the mesh, and bridge it to the cloud via MQTT for real-time remote monitoring.

**Who made it:** [Hendra Kusumah](https://www.hackster.io/hendra)

### A Self-Sustaining Meshtastic Node Family

<p style={{textAlign: 'center'}}><img src="https://files.seeedstudio.com/wiki/wiki%20images/3d%20Enclosure/6.23dimage5.png" alt="A Self-Sustaining Meshtastic Node Family" width={800} height="auto" /></p>

<p style={{textAlign: 'center', fontSize: '0.85rem'}}><strong>Source:</strong> <a href="https://www.hackster.io/jrbanks777/seeedpod-ultimate-77168f" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">SEEEDpod Ultimate</a></p>

A modular Meshtastic node ecosystem built around Wio Tracker L1, designed for off-grid deployment, urban portability, and everyday backup use with self-sustaining power options.

**Who made it:** [Jack](https://www.hackster.io/jrbanks777)

### Dual Wio Tracker L1 Portable Solar Station

<p style={{textAlign: 'center'}}><img src="https://files.seeedstudio.com/wiki/wiki%20images/3d%20Enclosure/6.23dimage6.png" alt="Dual Wio Tracker L1 Portable Solar Station" width={800} height="auto" /></p>

<p style={{textAlign: 'center', fontSize: '0.85rem'}}><strong>Source:</strong> <a href="https://www.hackster.io/fernando-doutel/dual-wio-tracker-l1-portable-solar-station-e3fcc1" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Dual Solar Charging Station for Wio Tracker L1</a></p>

A rugged portable solar station designed to charge two Wio Tracker L1 nodes anywhere, while also enabling Grove sensor integration for off-grid monitoring and emergency communication.

**Who made it:** [Fernando Doutel](https://www.hackster.io/fernando-doutel)

## E-ink

### Meshy: A Rugged 3D-Printed Case for Wio Tracker L1 E-ink

<p style={{textAlign: 'center'}}><img src="https://files.seeedstudio.com/wiki/wiki%20images/3d%20Enclosure/6.23dimage7.png" alt="Meshy 3D-printed case for Wio Tracker L1 E-ink" width={800} height="auto" /></p>

<p style={{textAlign: 'center', fontSize: '0.85rem'}}><strong>Source:</strong> <a href="https://www.hackster.io/akashedamana/meshy-wio-tracker-l1-e-ink-3d-printed-case-affad1" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">meshy-wio-tracker-l1-e-ink-3d-printed-case-affad1</a></p>

This is a 3D-printed protective case for the Wio Tracker L1 E-ink, designed to improve durability, portability, and mounting convenience for outdoor and everyday carry use. Its parametric design supports different battery sizes and mounting structures, and includes a clip-on mount for belts, backpacks, or MOLLE gear.

**Who made it:** [Akash](https://www.hackster.io/akashedamana)

**Where to download:** [a360](https://a360.co/3KNHxqQ)

### MeshPack Pro L1

<p style={{textAlign: 'center'}}><img src="https://files.seeedstudio.com/wiki/wiki%20images/3d%20Enclosure/6.23dimage8.png" alt="MeshPack Pro L1" width={800} height="auto" /></p>

<p style={{textAlign: 'center', fontSize: '0.85rem'}}><strong>Source:</strong> <a href="https://www.hackster.io/alleycat/acos-meshpack-pro-l1-for-meshtastic-1ec031" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">[ACOS] MeshPack Pro L1 for Meshtastic</a></p>

A rugged, modular 3D-printed enclosure for Wio Tracker L1, designed to turn the board into a more durable and customizable handheld Meshtastic device.

**Who made it:** [Alley Cat](https://www.hackster.io/alleycat)

### Solar Off-Grid Mesh Communicator

<p style={{textAlign: 'center'}}><img src="https://files.seeedstudio.com/wiki/wiki%20images/3d%20Enclosure/6.23dimage9.png" alt="Solar Off-Grid Mesh Communicator" width={800} height="auto" /></p>

<p style={{textAlign: 'center', fontSize: '0.85rem'}}><strong>Source:</strong> <a href="https://www.hackster.io/bastiaan-slee/meshtastic-20-25-cd4c1f" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Meshtastic 20.25</a></p>

A self-contained off-grid Meshtastic communicator built around Wio Tracker L1, combining a mini keyboard, E-ink display, vibration alerts, and rear solar charging for portable everyday and outdoor use.

**Who made it:** [Bastiaan Slee](https://www.hackster.io/bastiaan-slee)

### SkySense Flying Lab
