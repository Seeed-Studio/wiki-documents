Seeed Fusion offers logo, packaging, firmware, and accessory customization for standard Seeed hardware products. These options help adapt products to your brand, application, or deployment requirements.

This guide covers customization options, ordering, sample approval, and costs. For supported products, available options, current pricing, and lead times, refer to the service page and the relevant product pages.

## Customization Options

| Option | What it includes |
| --- | --- |
| Logo customization | Add your branding through methods such as screen printing or laser engraving. The method depends on the product and selected customization option. |
| Packaging customization | Customize packaging boxes, labels, sleeves, manuals, and outer-carton information. |
| Firmware customization | Preload firmware tested by Seeed before shipment, or adjust basic settings supported by the product. |
| Accessory options | Add or remove existing, tested accessories from the options available for the product. |

Available options vary by product. Visit the [Light Customization service page](https://www.seeedstudio.com/fusion_branding_firmware.html) to access the relevant product page and review its options.

For products not listed, firmware you have developed, or requirements not covered by the online options, contact [fusion@seeed.io](mailto:fusion@seeed.io) for assessment.

## Ordering and Sample Approval

Online light customization orders are submitted through the customization page for the selected product.

| Step | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Choose a product | Select the Seeed product you want to customize from the Light Customization service page. |
| Choose customization options | Review and select the logo, packaging, firmware, or accessory options supported by the product. |
| Enter quantities and requirements | Enter the quantities and customization details requested on the page. |
| Submit customization files | Upload the files needed for the selected options and add any relevant notes. |
| Review the order and pay | Check the order details, charges, and applicable terms, then complete payment. |

A physical sample lets you check colors, logo appearance, the look and feel of the packaging, label placement, and overall appearance. Samples are charged separately.

After sample approval, production follows the confirmed requirements. Specific sample arrangements and approval requirements are subject to the order, applicable terms, and project agreement.

## Cost Breakdown

Light customization costs generally include the standard product, non-recurring engineering fees for the selected options, and customization work.

| Cost | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Product cost | The cost of the selected standard hardware product. |
| Non-recurring engineering (NRE) fees | One-time engineering fees for work such as project assessment, setup, tooling, or production preparation. |
| Processing fees | Charges for customization work, based on the number of units. |

Each selected customization option has its own charges. Sample costs are calculated separately. Reorders with the same customization requirements generally do not incur additional NRE fees. The applicable charges are specified in the project quotation.

For current pricing, quantity requirements, payment arrangements, and lead times, refer to the relevant customization page and pricing policy.
