## Product & Service {#product-and-service}

### Does reComputer AI R2000 support a pre-configured hardware and firmware solution with an external GPIO, a built-in 26 TFLOPS Hailo 10 module and fanless cooling? Is there a standard or custom product that meets this? {#ai-r2000-sku100020831-gpio-hailo10}

**Applies to:** reComputer AI R2000 series and projects that need a custom pre-configured hardware and firmware solution

**Last verified:** 2026-11-01

The enclosure of reComputer AI R2000 does not offer a general-purpose GPIO; it only provides interfaces such as DI/DO, RS485 and RS232. The currently recommended solution, the reComputer AI R2130-12, comes with an internal Raspberry Pi 40-pin GPIO and a built-in Hailo NPU.
