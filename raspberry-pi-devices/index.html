Edge Devices Powered By Raspberry Pi

Introduction​

Welcome to the cutting-edge world of Raspberry Pi-powered devices at Edge Solution. Here, we explore the innovative solutions crafted by Seeed Studio for edge computing, specifically tailored for Raspberry Pi CM4/CM5 and Raspberry Pi 4/5. These customizable devices include Human-Machine Interface (HMI) units, versatile edge controllers, and AI suite boxes. Join us on a journey through the intricate landscape of these powerful devices, designed to enhance your projects with seamless and efficient edge computing capabilities.

Edge AI Computers​

Seeed Studio reComputer Industrial R & AI R Series – Rugged, Palm-Sized Edge AI Controllers Built for IIoT and Smart Automation

Powered by the high-performance Raspberry Pi 5 / Compute Module 5 and paired with a Hailo-8 AI accelerator, the reComputer Industrial R and AI R lines deliver up to 26 TOPS of compute muscle for real-time, multi-stream vision analytics and deep-learning inference right at the edge.

Industrial-grade connectivity comes standard: dual Gigabit Ethernet, isolated RS-485/RS-232, multi-channel DI/DO, plus M.2 and mini-PCIe slots that let you drop in 4G/5G, LoRa®, or NVMe SSDs in seconds. A fan-less, all-metal chassis guarantees silent, dust-proof operation from –20 °C to 60 °C, while hardware watchdog and optional UPS super-capacitor keep mission-critical tasks alive 24/7.

DIN-rail or wall-mount ready, the reComputer R series slides effortlessly into smart buildings, Industry 4.0 machine-vision cells, remote asset-control cabinets, and distributed video-analytics networks—converting protocols, collecting data, and running AI models where the action happens.

Edge Controllers​

Seeed Studio’s reComputer R and Edge Box families are rugged edge controllers engineered for industrial IoT and intelligent automation. Built around the high-performance Raspberry Pi Compute Module (CM4/CM5), they deliver quad-core Arm horsepower and up to 16 GB RAM. Dual Gigabit Ethernet (PoE-ready), isolated RS-485/RS-232, plus M.2/mini-PCIe bays for 4G/5G, LoRa®, or NVMe SSDs cover every IIoT, protocol-gateway, and edge-AI requirement. A fan-less, all-metal enclosure rated for –30 °C to +70 °C, hardware watchdog, and optional UPS super-capacitors guarantee reliable 24/7 operation in harsh environments. DIN-rail or wall-mount ready, they slide effortlessly into smart buildings, energy-management systems, and remote-control applications.

Raspberry Pi Original​

Raspberry Pi (aka "RPi") was originally designed and created by a non-profit organization with the intention to promote teaching computer science at an affordable cost. Several generations and models of Raspberry Pis have now been released that have expanded their footprints, power and applications.
