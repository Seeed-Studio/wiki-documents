Scenario Recommendations​

1. Learning & Prototyping​

  • Tight budget / first Jetson: reComputer J1010 / J1020 v2 (Jetson Nano).
  • Want Orin performance: reComputer J3010 (Orin Nano).

2. Edge AI Inference & Video Analytics​

  • Higher compute with mature ecosystem: reComputer J4011 (Orin NX).
  • Compact chassis with essential interfaces: reComputer Super J3010 / J4011.

3. Industrial Control & Automation​

  • Wide temp, anti-vibration, industrial I/O: reComputer Industrial J3010 / J4011 or reServer Industrial J3010 / J4011.
  • Both support DIN rail / VESA mounting and offer RS485, GPIO, PoE.

4. Robotics & AMR​

  • General robotics platform: reComputer Robotics J3010 / J4011, with multiple CAN and XT30 power.
  • Multi GMSL2 cameras: reComputer Robotics J3010/4011 with GMSL extension board.

5. In-Vehicle, Outdoor & Harsh Environments​

  • Waterproof/dustproof and automotive connectors: reComputer Rugged J3011 / J4012, IP66, M12 connectors.

6. High-end Autonomy / Large Compute R&D​

  • Need AGX Orin 32GB/64GB: reComputer Robotics J5011/5012 with GMSL, with 1G/10G networking and GMSL.
